# Imagery

No stock photography is bundled: images must be properly licensed, and none could be downloaded in the build environment.
Until you add files, each image slot shows a neutral dark placeholder (never a broken image).

Add JPG files to `public/images/` using these exact names (or change names in `src/config/images.ts`).
Recommended: 2400px wide for full-width slots, 1600px otherwise, JPEG quality 80-85, landscape unless noted. Next.js serves AVIF/WebP automatically.

Licensing: use images you own or that carry a licence permitting commercial use (e.g. Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay licences, or a paid stock library).
Do not use photos showing identifiable operator livery or logos unless you have permission, because the site must not imply that partner aircraft are your own fleet.
Keep a record of each image's source and licence.
