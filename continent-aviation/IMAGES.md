# Imagery

Every image on the site is a **real photograph** (sourced from Wikimedia Commons or NASA), listed below with its
licence. The earlier computer-generated renders have been removed. The page-header slots are mapped to these
files in `src/config/images.ts`. Aircraft photos show the aircraft TYPE and are labelled as illustrative; the site
never presents them as a specific aircraft offered.

If you replace a photo, keep its licence and credit up to date, use images you own or that permit commercial use,
and avoid identifiable operator livery or logos unless you have permission.
