The problem

When a crop looks wrong, help is often days away.

Most farmers cannot get a trained eye on a sick plant quickly. Extension officers cover many villages, apps often speak only a few languages, and signal is patchy in the field. A wrong guess means spraying the wrong thing, or waiting until it is too late.

How it works

Look, ask, then act: the way an agriculture officer works.

1

Photograph

Snap the affected leaf, or describe it by voice in your own language. Pick the crop to narrow the possibilities.

2

Answer a few questions

Where on the plant? How fast is it spreading? Short tap-to-answer questions separate look-alike problems that a photo alone cannot.

3

Get a clear next step

See the likely cause, what to check, and options, in plain words, read aloud if you prefer. Always confirm with your local KVK before spraying.

Our principles

Built to be trusted, not just fast.

  • It says "not sure" when it is not sure.A wrong confident answer is worse than no answer. Unclear cases point you to an expert.
  • Advice from official sources, shown.Our goal is that every treatment tip names its government or agriculture-university source.
  • No pesticide ads, no product pushing.Non-chemical and integrated pest-management options come first.
  • Your photos stay yours.We share photos for improving the service only with your clear consent.
  • Works for people who do not read much.Voice in, voice out, big icons, your language.
  • Honest about what it can do.We publish accuracy results, including the weak ones, once we have measured them.

Important: KisanSevak gives informational guidance. It is not a substitute for your local agriculture officer. Confirm any treatment, and especially any pesticide use, with your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) before applying it.

Roadmap

From borrowed AI to our own model, one tested step at a time.

We only move to the next step when the previous one passes a real test, so farmers never get worse advice along the way.

  1. Measure the baselineScore today's AI on labelled field photos so we know where we stand.
  2. Collect licensed field photos for the major cropsOpen, properly licensed data first, then expert-verified photos from partners.
  3. Train and test our own modelIt must match or beat the baseline on photos it has never seen before it replaces anything.
  4. Build the expert answer libraryOfficial-source guidance for each problem, translated and reviewed by agronomists and native speakers.
  5. Run on the phone, pilot with KVKsA small model inside the browser works with poor or no signal. Pilot with partners and real farmers.
  6. Keep growingWith consent, farmers' photos confirmed by experts help the model learn more crops and local diseases.
